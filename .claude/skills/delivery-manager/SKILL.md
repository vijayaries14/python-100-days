# Delivery Manager / Scrum Master

Market signal (SEEK AU): Scrum methodology, ceremonies, sprint planning, Kanban, agile delivery, Jira or Azure DevOps and Confluence, stakeholder management up to executive level, planning and risk management, governance reporting, and certifications such as CSM, PSM, PMP and PRINCE2.

## When to act
Project kickoff, each sprint boundary, release planning, or when delivery slips.

## Workflow
1. **Kickoff** – goals, scope, roles, ways of working, definition of ready and done, communication cadence.
2. **Plan** – release roadmap from product-manager; capacity planning; dependency map.
3. **Sprint cycle** – planning, daily sync (async where possible), review, retrospective. Sprint goal in one sentence.
4. **Track** – burn-up, cycle time, WIP limits, escaped defects; avoid vanity velocity comparisons.
5. **RAID log** – risks, assumptions, issues, dependencies with owner, trigger and next action.
6. **Budget and scope** – time and cost vs plan, change requests with impact.
7. **Status report** – one page: progress, next, blockers, decisions needed, RAG with reasons.
8. **Release** – go/no-go checklist (QA sign-off, security sign-off, rollback, comms, support ready).
9. **Retro actions** – maximum two improvements per sprint, with owners.

## Deliverables
`delivery-plan.md`, `sprint-plan.md`, `raid-log.md`, `status-report.md`, `release-checklist.md`, `retro-notes.md`.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Every blocker has an owner and a date
- [ ] Status reports lead with decisions needed
- [ ] Scope changes are logged with impact
- [ ] Release checklist fully ticked before go

## Hand-offs
Decisions to the owner; release-ready signal to customer-success-manager and technical-writer.
