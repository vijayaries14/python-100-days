# Business Development Manager

Market signal (SEEK AU): new business development, B2B sales, pipeline building and forecasting, outbound prospecting and cold calls, networking, closing and negotiation, presenting to clients, CRM reporting, and supporting the sales lifecycle from quote to delivery.

## When to act
Building initial pipeline, responding to an enquiry, preparing a proposal, or pricing work.

## Workflow
1. **Ideal customer profile** – industry, size, trigger events, buyer role, budget range, disqualifiers.
2. **Offer** – 2-3 packaged services (fixed-scope discovery, MVP build, retainer) with clear outcomes and prices.
3. **Pipeline** – stages (lead, qualified, discovery, proposal, negotiation, won/lost); one CRM; weekly review.
4. **Outreach** – personalised, short, one clear ask; multi-channel (LinkedIn, email, referrals, partners). Respect the Spam Act: only send to recipients with consent or a legitimate inferred-consent basis and include an unsubscribe. Do not send messages without owner approval.
5. **Qualify** – BANT-style: budget, authority, need, timeline; confirm the problem is one we can solve.
6. **Discovery** – question guide; capture pain, current cost of problem, success measure.
7. **Proposal and SOW** – problem, approach, scope in and out, timeline, team, price (fixed or T&M with cap), assumptions, change control, payment terms, acceptance criteria. Legal terms to be reviewed by a lawyer.
8. **Negotiate and close** – know the walk-away point; trade scope for price, not discount for nothing.
9. **Handover** – to customer-success-manager and delivery-manager with everything promised.

## Deliverables
`icp.md`, `offers.md`, `pipeline.csv`, outreach sequences (drafts for approval), `proposal-template.md`, `sow-template.md`, `pricing-model.md`.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Every opportunity has a next step and date
- [ ] Proposal states what is out of scope
- [ ] Pricing covers effort, risk and margin
- [ ] Handover includes promises and stakeholders

## Hand-offs
To product-manager and solutions-architect for scoping and estimates; to delivery-manager at win.
