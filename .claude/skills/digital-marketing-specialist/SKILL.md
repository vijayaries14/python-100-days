# Digital Marketing Specialist

Market signal (SEEK AU ads and 2027 outlook for Australian digital marketing): SEO, paid search and social (Google/Meta), content, marketing automation and CRM data hygiene, conversion optimisation, analytics, campaign planning and reporting, with demand for demonstrated, data-backed impact.

## When to act
Before launch, to generate pipeline, or when traffic or conversion stalls.

## Workflow
1. **Positioning** – who it is for, problem, differentiator, proof; one-sentence value proposition and three message pillars.
2. **Website** – clear offer above the fold, services pages, case studies, contact path, speed and accessibility; coordinate with ux-ui-designer and fullstack-engineer.
3. **SEO** – keyword research by intent, technical audit (indexing, speed, schema), content clusters, internal links; track rankings and conversions, not just traffic.
4. **Content** – case studies, how-tos from real projects, founder POV on LinkedIn; calendar with owner and CTA per piece.
5. **Paid** – start small, one channel at a time, tight audiences, tested creatives; define cost-per-lead target before spending.
6. **Email and automation** – consented lists, welcome and nurture sequences, CRM sync.
7. **Analytics** – GA4 events, UTM standard, conversion definitions, dashboard; cross-check with CRM outcomes.
8. **Compliance** – Spam Act consent and unsubscribe, privacy policy and cookie consent, truthful claims.
9. **Report** – monthly: spend, leads, cost per lead, conversion, learnings, next tests.

## Deliverables
`positioning.md`, `content-calendar.md`, `seo-audit.md`, `campaign-briefs/`, `tracking-plan.md`, `monthly-report.md`.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Every campaign has a goal metric, budget cap and end date
- [ ] Tracking verified before spend begins
- [ ] Claims are provable
- [ ] Leads flow to the CRM with source attached

## Hand-offs
Qualified leads to business-development-manager; customer proof points from customer-success-manager; docs from technical-writer.
