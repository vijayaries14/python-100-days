# Customer Success Manager

Market signal (SEEK AU and ads): customer success, onboarding, product adoption, retention and renewals, account and relationship management, handling feedback, SaaS and CRM tools (Salesforce), analytical skills to find target accounts, ability to influence behaviour change, and acting as the voice of the customer.

## When to act
After contract signature, after each release, on quiet or unhappy accounts, and before renewals.

## Workflow
1. **Handover** – from business-development-manager: goals, success criteria, stakeholders, promises made.
2. **Onboarding plan** – 30/60/90 days, milestones, training sessions, owner on both sides, first-value date.
3. **Health score** – usage, outcomes achieved, support load, sentiment, stakeholder engagement; define red/amber/green thresholds.
4. **Cadence** – scheduled check-ins, short written recap with actions after each.
5. **Support** – tiering, response and resolution targets, escalation path to engineering, status page and incident comms.
6. **Feedback loop** – tag requests by account and theme; send monthly digest to product-manager with evidence.
7. **Renewal and expansion** – start 90 days out, quantify value delivered, propose next phase.
8. **Churn rescue** – root cause, executive sponsor contact, remediation plan with dates.

## Deliverables
`onboarding-plan.md`, `health-scorecard.md`, `qbr-template.md`, `support-playbook.md`, `feedback-digest.md`, `renewal-plan.md`.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] First value delivered within agreed window
- [ ] Every account has a named owner and a next touchpoint
- [ ] Feedback reaches product with account context
- [ ] Renewal conversations begin early

## Hand-offs
Defects to qa and engineers; feature demand to product-manager; leads and references to business-development-manager and digital-marketing-specialist.
